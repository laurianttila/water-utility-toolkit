# Sociotechnical Diagnosis Canvases
## English versions will be added gradually
A set of three A2-sized workshop templates used to facilitate sociotechnical mapping of the utility and its perceived threat landscape. 
* **Sociotechnical Lens**: Treats the utility as an inseparable mix of technology and people.
* **Deep-Dive Analysis**: Helps non-experts identify high-priority risks often missed by purely technical lenses.

## How to use the canvases
### Sociotechnical mapping canvas / Toimntaympäristö (Finnish)
Use this canvas to identify 1) all the people (inlcuding subcontractors and other parties), 2) all technical elements of the utility (digital, physical), 3) relevant regulation, and 4) critical points in the process which depend on human and technical cooperation.
The results is an inventory of the utilities' sociotechnical environment (what needs to be protected).

## 📖 Background
A proof-of-concept toolkit of the canvases was co-developed with a small Finnish water utility in November-December 2025.
Canvases will be iterated when they are tested with more utilities.
